01
Image augment

Re-render an Enscape output as photoreal

Twelve of thirteen held the massing and the camera. gpt-image-1.5 drifted the cantilever — the first instance of a pattern that repeats.

Instruction given to every model

"Render this scene as a photorealistic architectural visualization, preserving the exact building massing and camera angle: the curved-top glass tower on the left, the fin-clad tower behind it, and the mixed-height podium with retail signage zones and landscaped terraces below. Match the existing materiality (glass curtain wall, bronze/gold metal cladding, white vertical fins on the right-hand towers), daylight lighting, shadows, and street-level activity (vehicles, planting, signage)."

↔
Source
The read
All eight expansion candidates held massing and camera without the cantilever drift seen in gpt-image-1.5. Grok Imagine 2.0 delivers top-tier crispness at extreme speed; FLUX.2 Max sets the standard for daylight speculars and warm bronze reflectivity; Seedream 5.0 Lite and Qwen Image 3 give dependable geometry holding at low cost. Both MAI models returned flawless 1:1 camera and massing fidelity.

03
Image augment

Swap the mood — blue hour, fog, cinematic uplift

Atmosphere is close to solved. Every expansion model delivered dusk, fog and wet reflections; gpt-image-1.5 lost the geometry again doing it.

Instruction given to every model

"Uplift this render into a cinematic photorealistic visualization, preserving the building massing and camera angle. Set the atmosphere to blue hour — deep blue-violet sky, low ambient light, building facades and signage glowing warmly against the cool dusk light. Add heavy, dense atmospheric fog rolling through the street and lower levels, thick enough to soften and partially obscure the base of the towers and diffuse the light from signage and windows. Interior floors should read as inhabited — visible variation in window lighting, hints of interior activity such as figures, furniture silhouettes, or desk lamps, rather than uniform flat glow. Wet pavement with reflections of the glowing facades and lights. Push contrast and mood to be highly dramatic and cinematic."

↔
Source
The read
No model in the initial batch hit all six checklist items, and gpt-image-2 garbled the signage text. FLUX.2 Max produced the best atmospheric lighting and interior occupancy of the entire benchmark with zero drift; Grok Imagine 2.0 is nearly 3× faster; Seedream 5.0 Lite and Qwen Image 3 hold geometry cheaply; both MAI models rival FLUX for mood, with layered fog and varied inhabited window lighting.
04
Image augment

Swap two materials, change nothing else

Thirteen of thirteen executed both substitutions and held the rest of the materiality. The finding here was geometry, not materials.

Instruction given to every model

"Keep the building geometry, camera angle, composition, and all other materials unchanged. Make two material changes: 1) Replace the gold/bronze vertical fin cladding on the right-hand podium volume with iridescent chromic perforated metal panels, maintaining the same panel/fin proportions. 2) Replace the signage wall's pink/mauve stone cladding (ground-level, left of centre) with hot-pink glossy plastic cladding, keeping the signage lettering and layout unchanged. Maintain existing daylight lighting throughout, and keep all other materials unchanged."

↔
Source
The read
Every expansion model performed both substitutions while keeping the building intact. Grok Imagine 2.0 and FLUX.2 Max rendered the most convincing iridescent cladding without disturbing adjacent curtain wall; both MAI models produced surgically clean boundary masks with vibrant specular reflections. gpt-image-1.5 changed the podium again — its third drift across four tasks.
05
Image augment

Zoom into one terrace without redesigning it

The task that separates the field. Five of thirteen held the constraint; the rest quietly re-rendered the whole elevation or invented a new building.

Instruction given to every model

"This is a resolution and detail upscale, not a redesign. Zoom into the highlighted rooftop terrace area exactly as it appears in the source image — do not alter the roofline, canopy shapes, terrace levels, steps, or any existing architectural geometry. Add resolution and richness only: realistic planting, paving detail, people using the space, and sculptural art pieces placed within the existing terrace geometry. The underlying architecture must be recognizably the same design as the source, just rendered at higher detail and closer proximity."

↔
Source
The read
Constrained detail development does not track with quality or geometry performance elsewhere — which is exactly why it earns its own task. Seedream 5.0 Lite joins gpt-image-2 as a true, exact crop-and-upscale of the specified terrace; both MAI models did the same, holding curved bronze soffits, stepped seating, glass balustrades and scale figures. FLUX.2 Max held the podium setback terraces. Seedream Pro and both Qwen models re-rendered the full elevation instead of cropping; Nano Banana 2 produced an unrelated building entirely.

05 / Method

How a model gets on this page.

Measured, not judged

  • Generation time, per run
  • File size — proxy for detail and compression
  • Cost per generation, from vendor pricing
  • Success rate — usable on first try
  • Retry rate — regenerations to acceptable
  • Output resolution vs required minimum
  • Artifact rate — counted, not rated

Scored 1–5 by reviewers

  • Instruction adherence
  • Architectural accuracy — proportion, scale, detail
  • Style consistency with previous outputs
  • Edit fidelity — did it preserve what should not change
  • Constraint-holding — did it stay inside the boundary

Run blind where possible. Minimum two reviewers, scores averaged. Raw outputs kept so runs stay auditable.

Integrate if it

  • Matches or beats baseline on qualitative average
  • Matches or beats baseline on success and artifact rate
  • Is neutral-or-better on speed and cost — or the quality gain justifies the delta
  • Holds constraints reliably on augment tasks, not just surface quality

Otherwise: park it, note why, revisit at the next model refresh.

Caveat worth stating plainly: one run per model per task. The gpt-image-1.5 geometry pattern repeated across three tasks, which is why it is called a pattern — single-task results are indicative, not settled.
