01 / The short answer

Six picks, depending on what you are protecting.

Surface quality and constraint-holding are separate skills, and now measurably so: across these thirteen models the two scores correlate at 0.108 — which is to say, not at all. The strongest-looking outputs were often the ones that quietly redesigned the building. There is no single ranking to give you, so choose on the axis you cannot afford to lose.

Following orders, and looking good, are two different skills.

Every output is graded twice. A vision model checks it against each explicit requirement of the instruction it was given — that is the horizontal axis. The vertical axis is a human eye on the same image. A model can sit far right and low: it did exactly as it was told, and the result is lifeless.

01
Image augment

Re-render an Enscape output as photoreal

Five models re-render this scene without touching the building. The rest pay for their photorealism somewhere — usually by dollying the camera in or quietly re-cladding a tower.

Instruction given to every model

"Render this scene as a photorealistic architectural visualization, preserving the exact building massing and camera angle: the curved-top glass tower on the left, the fin-clad tower behind it, and the mixed-height podium with retail signage zones and landscaped terraces below. Match the existing materiality (glass curtain wall, bronze/gold metal cladding, white vertical fins on the right-hand towers), daylight lighting, shadows, and street-level activity (vehicles, planting, signage)."

↔
Source
The read
Five clean sweeps — gpt-image-2, both Seedreams and both MAI models — and only two of those five also look the part. gpt-image-2 and Seedream 5.0 Pro take 100 and a 5 for craft; the other three hold the building perfectly and return something closer to a tidied render than a photograph. The failures are instructive: Nano Banana 2 scores 60.7 not for a wobbly line but for re-cladding the left tower's glass curtain wall in bronze fins, and gpt-image-1.5 lands at 50.0 having moved the camera and rebuilt the podium. FLUX.2 Max is the sharpest illustration of the split — the best daylight in the task, rated 4, on a building whose towers it reproportioned, scoring 71.4.
02
Image augment

Turn the render back into a concept sketch

Which hand you want is a preference. Whether you got a drawing at all is not — and two models returned the render with a wash over it.

Instruction given to every model

"Convert this render into a loose hand-drawn/marker concept sketch, preserving the composition and massing exactly. Muted tones, visible linework, concept-stage feel."

↔
Source
The read
Six models take a clean 100 here, and among those six the choice really is only which hand you want: gpt-image-2's ink and watercolour, Seedream 5.0 Pro's design-office marker, Nano Banana Pro's pen-and-ochre, Grok's marker, Seedream 5.0 Lite's technical line, MAI-Image-2.5 Pro's watercolour on parchment. But the task does rank the bottom. Nano Banana 2 Lite scores 66.7 because it did not convert the image — the render survives underneath a blue sky wash, glass reflections and photographic planting intact — and both Qwen models do a milder version of the same thing, laying graphite over a rendering rather than drawing. That is a failure to follow the instruction, not a style you might happen to dislike.
03
Image augment

Swap the mood — blue hour, fog, cinematic uplift

Blue hour and warm glow are solved. Wet pavement and genuinely dense fog are not — five models leave the street bone dry, and only one model in thirteen hits every requirement.

Instruction given to every model

"Uplift this render into a cinematic photorealistic visualization, preserving the building massing and camera angle. Set the atmosphere to blue hour — deep blue-violet sky, low ambient light, building facades and signage glowing warmly against the cool dusk light. Add heavy, dense atmospheric fog rolling through the street and lower levels, thick enough to soften and partially obscure the base of the towers and diffuse the light from signage and windows. Interior floors should read as inhabited — visible variation in window lighting, hints of interior activity such as figures, furniture silhouettes, or desk lamps, rather than uniform flat glow. Wet pavement with reflections of the glowing facades and lights. Push contrast and mood to be highly dramatic and cinematic."

↔
Source
The read
MAI-Image-2.5 Pro is the only clean sweep of the nine criteria. The two that separate the field are the ones easiest to skip: a genuinely wet street, which Nano Banana 2, Nano Banana 2 Lite, Nano Banana Pro, Seedream 5.0 Pro and Qwen Image 3 all omit entirely, and fog thick enough to actually obscure the tower bases rather than haze the background. The instruction's most specific demand — interiors that read as inhabited, with variation rather than a flat glow — is where the Nano Banana models are strongest; Pro's are the best in the benchmark. FLUX.2 Max is worth reading closely against its own reputation: superb wet reflections and interior depth, rated 5, but pushed past blue hour into night, with fog that never thickens and a podium that has grown extra volumes. 79.4, not the clean sheet.
04
Image augment

Swap two materials, change nothing else

Thirteen of thirteen performed both substitutions. Only two did nothing else — which makes this the hardest task in the benchmark, not the easiest.

Instruction given to every model

"Keep the building geometry, camera angle, composition, and all other materials unchanged. Make two material changes: 1) Replace the gold/bronze vertical fin cladding on the right-hand podium volume with iridescent chromic perforated metal panels, maintaining the same panel/fin proportions. 2) Replace the signage wall's pink/mauve stone cladding (ground-level, left of centre) with hot-pink glossy plastic cladding, keeping the signage lettering and layout unchanged. Maintain existing daylight lighting throughout, and keep all other materials unchanged."

↔
Source
The read
Doing the two swaps is easy; the instruction's real test is the clause that says change nothing else, and it has the lowest mean score of the five tasks at 75.1. Only gpt-image-2 and Nano Banana 2 Lite are genuinely surgical — both swaps executed, every other surface left alone. FLUX.2 Max spread iridescence across four further podium volumes that were bronze and white in the source. Grok painted three. Qwen Image 3 added a second hot-pink wall the instruction never mentioned, and MAI-Image-2.5 ran the pink several storeys up past the low stone wall it was meant for. None of that is visible in a pass mark; all of it is visible the moment you put source and output in one frame.
05
Image augment

Zoom into one terrace without redesigning it

Five models return an exact crop of the marked terrace. The rest range from enlarging the ledge into a plaza to inventing a rooftop garden on a different building in a different city.

Instruction given to every model

"This is a resolution and detail upscale, not a redesign. Zoom into the highlighted rooftop terrace area exactly as it appears in the source image — do not alter the roofline, canopy shapes, terrace levels, steps, or any existing architectural geometry. Add resolution and richness only: realistic planting, paving detail, people using the space, and sculptural art pieces placed within the existing terrace geometry. The underlying architecture must be recognizably the same design as the source, just rendered at higher detail and closer proximity."

↔
Source
The read
Five exact crops: gpt-image-2, Qwen Image 3 Pro, MAI-Image-2.5, MAI-Image-2.5 Pro and Nano Banana 2 Lite, each holding the curved bronze soffit, the fluted white cylinders beneath it and the terrace levels, adding only the planting, paving, people and sculpture asked for. Two earlier calls need correcting against the images: Qwen Image 3 Pro was described here as re-rendering the full elevation and in fact delivers one of the cleanest zooms in the task, while Seedream 5.0 Lite, described as an exact crop, reshaped the canopy from a smooth swoop into a radial fan and turned the ledge into a circular plaza — good work, but not the constraint. At the bottom, Seedream 5.0 Pro did not zoom at all, Grok invented a structural fin that exists nowhere in the source, and gpt-image-1.5 produced a genuinely lovely roof garden belonging to another building, hills on the horizon included.
04 / Judging

06 / Method

How a model gets on this page.

Measured, not judged

  • Generation time, per run
  • File size — proxy for detail and compression
  • Cost per generation, from vendor pricing
  • Success rate — usable on first try
  • Retry rate — regenerations to acceptable
  • Output resolution vs required minimum
  • Artifact rate — counted, not rated

Scored 1–5 by reviewers

  • Instruction adherence
  • Architectural accuracy — proportion, scale, detail
  • Style consistency with previous outputs
  • Edit fidelity — did it preserve what should not change
  • Constraint-holding — did it stay inside the boundary

Run blind where possible. Minimum two reviewers, scores averaged. Raw outputs kept so runs stay auditable.

Integrate if it

  • Matches or beats baseline on qualitative average
  • Matches or beats baseline on success and artifact rate
  • Is neutral-or-better on speed and cost — or the quality gain justifies the delta
  • Holds constraints reliably on augment tasks, not just surface quality

Otherwise: park it, note why, revisit at the next model refresh.

Caveat worth stating plainly: one run per model per task. The gpt-image-1.5 geometry pattern repeated across three tasks, which is why it is called a pattern — single-task results are indicative, not settled.
